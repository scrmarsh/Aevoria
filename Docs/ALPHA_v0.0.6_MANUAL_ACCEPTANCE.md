# Alpha v0.0.6 manual acceptance

The automated report is [here](ALPHA_v0.0.6_TEST_RESULTS.md). These gates require physical operation and aesthetic judgment; scripted native captures do not mark them accepted.

- Open each original size plus Huge and Colossal. Cross land, coast, lake and river chunk edges at all zooms, moving in both directions and looking back. Check cold and warm views. Look for cracks, color rectangles, dark strips, duplicated water and transient openings. No fog/blur workaround is acceptable.
- Compare near/medium/far forest, open ground, mountain cliffs and coast to Sean's supplied concepts. Judge species silhouettes, spacing, partial foliage snow, fallen timber, sparse rocks and rare boulder scale. Procedural assets remain below reference fidelity.
- Leave a cold location, advance time and return. Snow and ice should grow gradually and persist after saving. Continue through warmth and confirm gradual thaw. No camera-dependent accumulation.
- Independently toggle keyboard, middle drag and edge scrolling. Combine enabled inputs without excess diagonal speed; check UI, typing, map/inventory, window boundary, focus loss and close navigation. Look straight up, fly through terrain with default collision off, and recenter without regenerating.
- Verify all six speed buttons change elapsed clock speed. Pause/resume, save and reload; retain speed and progression.
- Enter exploration on slopes and beside water, trees, logs, boulders and crates. Walk, sprint, crouch beneath a low opening, jump, change V view and adjust bob. Ensure terrain contact and safe recovery. Natural-detail physical collision remains incomplete.
- Harvest eligible wood/stone with the required tools, leave/revisit/reload, and confirm depletion. Fund a test crate, place it on nearby ground, collide with it and reload it.
- Dig supported dry low-relief ground. Inspect the real opening, remaining solid below, surface connection, collider and saved edits. Unsupported patch borders should refuse edits. Local cavity/roof support is experimental; do not expect unrestricted cave traversal.
- M map: compare actual positions/coasts/rivers/lakes; zoom/pan, select a location and inspect coordinate/heading labels. Test circle/square/rectangle minimap, each corner, opacity, size, orientation, resources and waypoint options. Save settings and restart.
- Change creation seed, world size, relief, water, rainfall and temperature; confirm the debounced preview and approximate statistics respond. Civilization suitability must remain labeled heuristic.
- F11 on every attached monitor: borderless native dimensions without a title bar, Alt-Tab/focus loss, return to remembered window size, 15-second Keep/Revert including timeout while unfocused. Navigate every menu and confirm display mode remains unchanged.
- Upgrade a schema-3 world through Upgrade copy. Check original directory bytes unchanged, new identity, same geography/ecology/time, new seasonal epoch and usable new saves. Never approve silent regeneration or resetting old ecology.
- Run an extended session with repeated worlds and loads, at least an hour of streaming, accelerated time, map changes, tool use and excavation. Check memory growth and retain frame outliers. Per-process VRAM and externally presented-frame timing remain unmeasured.
