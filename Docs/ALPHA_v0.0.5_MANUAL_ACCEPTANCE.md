# Alpha v0.0.5 — open physical and long-session acceptance

Automated scripted navigation and view tests are recorded in the [current report](ALPHA_v0.0.5_TEST_RESULTS.md). They do not replace physical-input acceptance. The following gates remain open; no checked boxes are implied.

- Create a Small, Medium and Large world through the actual controls. Inspect Recents and Load metadata; compare saved year, last played, seed and dimensions. Schema-2 worlds should report migration required without changing their files.
- Exercise WASD, Shift acceleration, RMB orbit, MMB pan and wheel zoom from ground level through overview. Travel over ridges with following and collision separately disabled/enabled. Confirm no unexpected bobbing or UI input leaking into camera movement.
- Toggle F3 and F4, select map modes, travel across region boundaries, pause/resume and vary time speed. Confirm inspection describes camera focus and counts are aggregate fractional populations. Submitted wildlife proxies need not all occupy visible pixels.
- Watch local deer, hares and wolves where habitat permits. Check resting, wandering, avoidance/pursuit and water rejection; record clipping, frozen poses, threshold appearance and other placeholder limits. Production animation is unfinished.
- Compare fire damage before and after its event ends, save/reload, and continue recovery over years. Floods currently affect ecology without dynamic inundation geometry; flame/smoke effects are absent.
- Save, duplicate, rename, unload and reload repeatedly. Check a compatible world's ecology persists and new worlds do not retain previous wildlife/material/texture resources. Do not use valuable archives for deliberate interruption/failure testing.
- Verify display preview/rollback, Settings persistence, Escape, OS close, Alt-Tab and autosave failures using physical input. Failed saves must leave the current world available.
- Run several real hours through multiple worlds while sampling memory, resource counts, frame pacing and application VRAM where supported. The current 60-second moving soak and accelerated centuries do not prove this gate.
- Investigate accelerated-time frame outliers. The final audit recorded 55.65 ms near a 1.30 ms GC marker; this does not establish that GC explains the entire interval. Earlier 24.10/28.78 ms outliers remain archived. The 60-FPS pacing gate remains open.
- Review procedural terrain, faceted trees, coastline steps, coarse lake/river ribbons, LOD changes, ecological cover boundaries and close wildlife against the supplied references. Capture unresolved gaps explicitly.

No public release or Alpha v0.0.6 social systems are declared by these checks.
