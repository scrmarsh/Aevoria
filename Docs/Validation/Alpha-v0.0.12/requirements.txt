# AEVORIA — ALPHA v0.0.12
## THE CHARACTER REMASTER HOTFIX
### Complete Codex Development Prompt

## MISSION

Develop **Aevoria Alpha v0.0.12 — The Character Remaster Hotfix** using Unity 6.3 LTS and URP.

This update is specifically focused on rebuilding and improving the playable Dr. House character, correcting character anatomy, fixing equipment orientation, and improving animations.

**Dr. House is the absolute highest priority.**

The current character resembles a creepy, blocky Roblox-style mannequin rather than a recognizable human. His arms bend inward unnaturally, his tools appear incorrectly oriented, his cane is in the wrong hand, and his limp is not convincingly animated.

These are not minor cosmetic problems. They require meaningful improvements to the actual 3D model, skeleton, rigging, equipment attachments, and animations.

The goal is to transform Dr. House into a recognizable, properly proportioned, realistically animated character.

This should remain a focused hotfix. Do not rebuild the terrain generator, hydrology, settlements, or world simulation during this update.

Preserve the existing v0.0.11 functionality and official release files.

---

# PHASE 1 — COMPLETE DR. HOUSE MODEL REMASTER

## 1. Facial reconstruction

Replace or substantially rebuild the current low-quality procedural face.

The character should have a recognizable resemblance to Dr. Gregory House as portrayed by Hugh Laurie, using the character reference images supplied previously.

Required improvements:

- Realistic human head proportions.
- Properly modeled forehead, brow, cheeks, cheekbones, and jaw.
- A naturally shaped nose.
- Properly positioned and proportioned eyes.
- Realistic eye sockets, eyelids, and eyebrows.
- A properly modeled mouth and lips.
- Natural ears and facial proportions.
- Subtle facial asymmetry.
- A mature facial appearance.
- Short salt-and-pepper hair.
- A believable hairline and hairstyle.
- Light salt-and-pepper facial stubble.
- Natural skin coloring and material variation.
- Facial expressions that suit his cynical personality.

**Do not use cubes, spheres, or primitive placeholder shapes as the final visible face.**

Use a genuinely modeled character mesh with appropriate topology, skin weights, normals, UVs, and materials.

Inspect the existing editable Blender assets. Rebuild them where necessary rather than preserving defective geometry.

If the original reference images are unavailable to Codex, request them or clearly report the limitation rather than claiming a recognizable likeness without reference validation.

## 2. Realistic body proportions

The current character has unnatural shoulders, arms, torso, and legs.

Rebuild his proportions to resemble a tall, slender adult man.

Requirements:

- Natural shoulder width.
- Proper chest and torso proportions.
- Realistic upper arms and forearms.
- Correct elbow locations.
- Proper wrist articulation.
- Fully modeled hands and fingers.
- Realistic hips, knees, ankles, and feet.
- Natural neck and head alignment.
- No blocky rectangular limbs.
- No disconnected joints.
- No visible gaps or clipping between body segments.
- No unnaturally large or small body parts.

The model must hold up visually from first-person, third-person, and reverse third-person perspectives.

## 3. Clothing overhaul

Preserve his established outfit:

- Navy-blue blazer.
- Light-blue open-collar shirt.
- Navy trousers.
- Dark shoes.

Improve the outfit with:

- Realistic blazer proportions and lapels.
- Natural shirt collar and neckline.
- Convincing sleeve construction.
- Appropriate fabric folds.
- Better trouser geometry.
- Properly modeled shoes.
- Improved fabric textures and material properties.
- Natural clothing movement during animation.

Avoid making the clothes look painted onto a rectangular body.

# PHASE 2 — CORRECT CANE AND TOOL HANDEDNESS

## 4. Cane MUST be in the right hand

**Dr. House must hold his walking cane in his RIGHT hand.**

The existing left-hand cane placement is incorrect.

Requirements:

- Attach the cane to the right-hand grip.
- Correct its orientation.
- Properly align the fingers around the handle.
- Ensure the cane does not clip through his wrist or body.
- Keep the cane on his right side while standing and walking.
- Use believable cane positioning during turns and transitions.

The cane must not float beside the character or behave like a rigid decorative object.

## 5. Dr. House is the ONLY left-handed tool user

**Every normal tool must be equipped in Dr. House's LEFT hand.**

This includes:

- Pickaxes.
- Shovels.
- Axes.
- Other handheld tools introduced later.

Unlike the other characters, Dr. House uses his left hand for tools because his right hand is reserved for his cane.

Make this a character-specific equipment configuration.

Do not globally reverse the handedness of all playable characters.

The default Explorer and Roman soldier should retain their normal right-handed tool configuration.

## 6. Correct tool orientation

The current pickaxe sometimes appears backward or incorrectly angled.

Fix:

- Tool grip positions.
- Handle orientation.
- Pickaxe head direction.
- Wrist alignment.
- Hand placement.
- Tool swing arcs.
- Equipment attachment transforms.
- Animation offsets.

A pickaxe must be held by its handle, with its head facing the correct direction for mining.

Tools must not point awkwardly behind the character or pass through his body.

## 7. Cane and tool interaction

Dr. House must be capable of carrying his cane in the right hand and using a tool in the left hand.

Implement character-specific animation blending or overrides so these actions work together.

During mining:

- The left hand correctly grips and swings the pickaxe.
- The right hand handles the cane appropriately.
- The torso responds naturally to the mining movement.
- The arms do not cross or collapse inward.
- The tool does not intersect the character's body.
- Equipment remains aligned throughout the animation.

Do not solve equipment conflicts by permanently removing the cane without explanation.

# PHASE 3 — GENUINE ANIMATED RIGHT-LEG LIMP

## 8. Implement an actual limp

Dr. House must have a clearly visible **RIGHT-LEG LIMP**.

The current implementation relies too heavily on presentational asymmetry rather than a convincing animated gait.

This must change.

Required animation characteristics:

- Uneven stride timing.
- Reduced or altered weight-bearing on the right leg.
- Shorter or less confident right-leg steps.
- Natural compensating motion in the left leg.
- Asymmetrical hip movement.
- Subtle torso lean and weight shifting.
- Appropriate shoulder movement.
- Realistic right-knee and ankle articulation.
- Proper foot contact with the ground.

The limp must be visible from the front, side, and rear.

It should be recognizable while the character is actually moving, not just when examining a static pose.

## 9. Cane-assisted walking

The cane needs a coordinated animation cycle.

Requirements:

- Animate the right arm independently.
- Coordinate cane placement with the right-leg gait.
- Move the cane forward naturally.
- Contact the ground at appropriate points in the walking cycle.
- Apply believable weight shifts during support.
- Prevent the cane from passing through terrain.
- Adapt contact to slopes and uneven ground.

Use animation curves, inverse kinematics, and ground-contact checks where appropriate.

The cane should provide the appearance of physical support. It does not need to simulate every real-world force, but movement must be spatially coherent.

## 10. Walking and running

Create character-specific locomotion for Dr. House.

His movements should reflect his right-leg impairment rather than reuse the Roman soldier's ordinary walking animation.

Include:

- Idle standing with cane.
- Slow walking.
- Regular walking.
- Faster movement or running.
- Turning.
- Starting and stopping.
- Walking uphill and downhill.
- Uneven terrain movement.
- Mining and digging transitions.

The limp should remain recognizable where appropriate.

Avoid excessive exaggeration that makes his movement comical or physically impossible.

Synchronize foot animations with actual movement speed to prevent sliding.

# PHASE 4 — FIX ARM AND HAND ANIMATION

## 11. Natural resting posture

The current humanoid characters sometimes bend their arms inward as if they are trying to put their hands together.

This must be corrected.

Requirements:

- Arms resting naturally near the sides.
- Correct shoulder rotation.
- Appropriate elbow bending.
- Correct forearm orientation.
- Relaxed hands when not using tools.
- Realistic wrist positioning.
- No arms collapsing inward.
- No repeated clipping through the torso.
- No rigid mannequin-like idle stance.

Dr. House should have a relaxed but slightly asymmetrical standing posture due to his cane.

## 12. Improve the character rig

Inspect the current skeleton and animation system for:

- Incorrect bone orientations.
- Invalid bind poses.
- Improper humanoid retargeting.
- Bad shoulder and elbow constraints.
- Incorrect wrist rotations.
- Tool-socket orientation problems.
- Poor skin weights.
- Animation blending conflicts.
- Scale inconsistencies.
- Incorrect left/right handedness assumptions.

Correct the underlying causes rather than compensating with arbitrary visual offsets.

## 13. Repair the Roman soldier

Although Dr. House remains the highest priority, also fix the existing Roman soldier's animation defects.

Required corrections:

- Natural arm positioning.
- Correct elbow and shoulder movement.
- Right-handed tool usage.
- Correct pickaxe orientation.
- Proper mining and digging animations.
- Natural walking and running.
- Improved idle posture.
- No twisted wrists or backward equipment.

Do not alter the Roman soldier to use the Doctor's limp, cane, or reversed tool handling.

## 14. Preserve other playable characters

The default Explorer must remain functional.

Do not introduce regressions to:

- Walking.
- Running.
- Jumping.
- Flight.
- Freefall.
- Mining.
- Digging.
- Character selection.
- Camera modes.

# FINAL DIRECTIVE

**The purpose of Alpha v0.0.12 is to make Dr. House look and move like Dr. House.**

His current appearance is unacceptable as a finished character.

The new model must have proper human anatomy, recognizable facial features, realistic clothing, correct hands, a right-hand cane, left-handed tool usage, and a physically convincing animated right-leg limp.

At the same time, fix the Roman soldier's unnatural arm posture and backward-facing tools without introducing new character regressions.

Prioritize Dr. House above all other features.

**Do not expand this hotfix into another major world-generation update.**

Terrain refinement, broader NPC model improvements, smoother village roads, and additional environmental work belong in the following update.

The final acceptance standard is straightforward:

**Dr. House must no longer look like a Roblox-style placeholder. He must look, walk, and behave like a recognizable, believable human character in the actual game.**
