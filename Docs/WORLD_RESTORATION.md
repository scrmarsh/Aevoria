# World restoration architecture — Alpha v0.0.8

This targeted recovery retains the v0.0.7 planetary hierarchy, five exact 128–2048 km presets, climate, ecology, seasonal memory, wetness, persistence, menus, inspection and exploration. See the [pre-correction analysis](ALPHA_v0.0.8_REGRESSION_ANALYSIS.md) and [executed validation](ALPHA_v0.0.8_TEST_RESULTS.md). The WPF project and historical build folders are not recovery targets.

## Terrain and materials

`ContinuousGrid` provides shape-preserving cubic interpolation with shared node tangents. Geological fields retain the bounded 257-square analysis. Thermal transport has 32 bounded passes; a post-transport quantile corrects the reference datum against requested land coverage, independently of the user's ocean level. Connected uplift still controls highland detail; analytic valley/crest modulation gives broad belts local relief. This is not a physical erosion solver, and the relief setting remains a theoretical parameter rather than a guaranteed summit elevation.

The 768-square overview and one regional grid remain bounded. Regional quality selects 256/512/1024 cells; spacing follows projected view span in powers of two, with one cancellable worker, one explicitly counted staging mesh with native copies spread across frames, and atomic publication of matching mesh/height/normal fields. Its outer join scales with eight regional cells. Overview/regional normals describe the actual sampled geometry. Near chunk boundaries still share their complete common profile. Local tile discovery covers the frustum's ground-plane footprint and movement lookahead, within the existing `(2 × radius + 1)^2` budget and moving 64-square atlas. Two workers and one local mesh upload per frame remain explicit limits. Very oblique views and complex relief can still exceed a finite footprint budget.

Material noise bands are filtered individually using projected world derivatives, retaining stable world coordinates. Climatic moisture controls broad material zones rather than replacing it with coarse ecological cell moisture. Ecological forest/burn state still affects presentation. Climate textures retain reference elevation in half precision; weather also publishes its reference ground. Local lapse correction avoids painting a coarse temperature grid onto a finer mountain. Snow responds to elevation, temperature, exposure and slope; rock remains exposed on steeper faces. No fog or blur is used as a substitute for geometry/material correction.

## Water and ownership

Hydrology retains priority-flood receivers and accumulating discharge. Paths have 32 intervals. Fine path lows can lower downstream spill levels before lake construction. Per-path water profiles are monotone and shared by channel carving, surface geometry and bank queries. Width grows with discharge and caps at 280 m. Channel depth ranges from 0.5 to 6 m; dry valley grading is separate from the shallow bed. Nearby corridors blend outside the wetted channel, while its centre retains its own profile. A below-water natural depression or confluence can still be deeper than that nominal bed: tests and native evidence must distinguish those cases.

Rejected-basin breach repair is retained. Each accepted lake still has one connected field and level. Shore cells refine the shared physical height field with a combined one-million-sample budget and up to 16 subdivisions; fully wet patches merge into larger triangles. Geometry and ownership query those same samples. This improves bounded shoreline resolution without allocating full-world metre fields. Where the refinement budget is exhausted or physical scale is very large, shoreline detail remains limited. Narrow channels can still be undersampled by distant terrain; no unrelated blue overlay is introduced.

Water uses brighter depth ranges and distinct freshwater colors. Transparency quality 0 uses model depth; 1 uses rendered eye depth; 2 reconstructs the submerged world position from the actual scene depth. Continuous absorption and Fresnel reflection retain visible shallow geometry. The terrain shader shades submerged near-field banks as sediment/rock. This is not refractive fluid simulation. Aquatic habitat classifications remain environmental foundations; marine populations, diving and an underwater food web are excluded.

## Vegetation, clouds and excavation

Vegetation instance bounds use actual height ranges rather than ending at a fixed 4200 m. Overhead visibility considers horizontal distance and camera height while respecting the selected distance budget. Existing placement and ecological coverage remain authoritative. Actual channel bank distance determines river water; dry graded valleys remain eligible habitat. Natural details use nearest outward rings, a 512-site cap and longer presentation visibility. Actual hot-desert resource sites present cacti, scrub or dry grass; this does not add a second ecosystem population. Up to 32 seeded submerged rocks reuse the existing boulder mesh in eligible shallow aquatic habitat; they are presentation, not harvestable marine populations.

Clouds use soft individual formations within forecast coverage. Shared formation code drives surface shadows. Advection follows world-day forecast wind at each latitude; higher cloud quality adds secondary puffs. The extended sheet has no visible world-rectangle edge in the playable domain. It remains layered procedural cloud art, not a volumetric simulation.

Solid excavation retains the same scalar subtraction, bounds, winding and analytic normals. Worker-side indexing merges repeated positions at a micrometre lookup tolerance, preserving original stored positions. A single Unity job pre-bakes each readable immutable mesh with the same cooking options used by its collider, following the documented [Physics.BakeMesh reuse contract](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Physics.BakeMesh.html). The installed mesh, collision and ground mask remain active until replacement publication. Closing waits for the owned bake before destroying its mesh. Native preparation, bake wait and main-thread assignment are measured separately; bake wait includes scheduling and is not isolated CPU time. A lower vertex count alone is not evidence that every hitch has disappeared.

## Follow-up diagnosis

The exact native Miniature/Continents field probe isolated remaining horizontal bands in climate rainfall rather than raw or carved heights. Climate 3 transports moisture with 60/20/20 percent upwind/crosswind exchange, retains local lift and rain-shadow loss, and blends prevailing direction through latitude boundaries. It does not blur final biome or terrain colors. Synthetic coastal-source tests check actual crosswind supply and deterministic output; native overview captures remain the visual check.

Drainage corridor lookup uses conservative expanded segment bounds and compact-support weights which reach zero at the candidate radius. A background weight preserves the original surface outside corridors. This removes lookup-cell steps while retaining actual channel centre profiles. Cloud quality zero disables both cloud geometry and its terrain/water shadows. Higher settings share the same warped, jittered formation field; visibly layered art remains a limitation.

Local tile interiors now derive slope at their actual LOD spacing, joining the common two-metre border stencil over two rows. Mixed-LOD border height/normal assertions remain intact. Native diagnostic captures isolated repeated dark bank dots: disabling direct shadows, water geometry and vegetation did not remove them, while flat local normals did. Flat normals were diagnostic only; production uses the sampled surface slope. The final native follow-up verifies that correction.

Hydrology 4 replaces the moving nearest-polyline window with a bounded search over all 32 intervals of a shape-preserving smooth centreline. Endpoint AABBs conservatively reject distant intervals; each candidate uses up to eight clamped Newton steps and checks its endpoints. The curve stays within its interval endpoint bounds. The same distance/profile query supplies carving and bank ownership; water levels use monotone shared-tangent interpolation through the retained downhill samples. Rendered water still approximates the curve with the existing 32 intervals. This removes the repeated broad-valley corner fans seen in the local 16 m field probe, while retaining finite sampling and confluence limitations.
