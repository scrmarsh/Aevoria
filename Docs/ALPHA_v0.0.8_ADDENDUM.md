Traveler horizontal validation now uses the actual world boundary. The altitude envelope is 50 km, covering the relief control and development flight. God-mode surface safety is unconditional; the former collision toggle requests wider slope probes. Navigation consults authoritative ground and the installed local/outgoing/regional/overview representation, including boundary morphing. Exact installed stitch triangles and their per-vertex morphs determine the presentation floor. A publication notification requests another clearance check before rendering; upward correction is immediate and downward release eases over time. Navigation and streaming footprint rays use angular projection coefficients rather than inverting a planetary far plane. The prior native candidate emitted 104 frustum-unprojection warnings at extreme clip ratios; the new regression compares ordinary and physical-camera rays against Unity at normal ratios and requires stable finite rays at 0.05 m / 8,000 km clipping. Near clipping follows actual clearance and a position-aware precision guard (approximately 0.32 m at 800 km coordinates). The guard stays within one quarter of God-mode terrain clearance. Ground views retain a 180 km far plane for the 140 km scenery, while overview clipping expands with current range. Exploration uses the same precision guard and a ground-view far plane. Tightening far clipping alone did not eliminate the observed Colossal warnings; the full precision-corrected Colossal sequence then completed with none. The short forest-motion A/B probe failed to reproduce them in either mode and is retained as inconclusive. Invalid navigation state recovers to a finite view without waiting for a terrain task. Existing movement inputs remain available. Zoom uses a reversible exponential target: one default normalized wheel notch changes distance by approximately 21.5%, with interpolation. Panning follows clearance with a separately configurable 60 m/s minimum, rather than scaling directly from orbit distance.

## Physical terrain and material diagnosis

The new native diagnostic exposes ordinary shading, geometry-only lighting, normals, unlit material color, elevation and material weights. Both `Diagnostics-before` and `Diagnostics-gradient` are retained. Fog and clouds are disabled for these comparisons. They show geometry shelves in the old mountain field and rectangular material variation over geometrically flat grassland. The worlds change under a new generator; selected peak/grassland locations differ and these are not identical-coordinate art comparisons.

The prior scalar field interpolated random lattice values in Cartesian axes, forcing zero derivatives on each lattice line. The new triangular gradient basis removes that construction constraint; it does not blur the existing terrain or add noise on top to conceal squares. Thermal transport distributes excess over eight slope-qualified neighbors, weighted by physical diagonal distance, instead of selecting one axial neighbor. Shape-preserving raster reconstruction and post-transport land-coverage calibration remain. A further native review exposed a nearest-plate ordering defect: the ridge axis reversed at the centre of the same tectonic boundary, reversing its warped cosine phase and producing sharp cliff seams. Each plate pair now has a stable orientation. The actual-boundary regression fails on the prior source (direction dot product -1). A second native inspection showed that changing the selected neighbor still stepped the uplift forcing. Soft memberships now blend all materially contributing plate pairs, including strength and each stable pair's ridge phase before rasterization. This removes the nearest-pair switch from the physical height construction; the geological raster gains one bounded float field (2.64 MB total).

A second source of angular geography was the 12-unit subtraction along equal continental-lobe values. That term excavated deep, narrow sea canals through connected interiors. It is removed; continental gaps now follow independently positioned landforms. Archipelago mode retains separately warped island domains and a resolved separation margin; a regression requires at least three substantial islands. The summit response approaches its upper envelope smoothly instead of clipping whole belts to an identical height. These wide sea channels must not be mistaken for river meshes. River receiver authority remains acyclic. Lowland meander is bounded laterally, vanishes with its derivative at endpoints, and retains positive progress along the receiver segment. Its same fitted centreline and downhill level drive carving, banks and water.

Material hashing now uses integer cell identities and triangular gradients. Filtering uses a shared world-space pixel scale rather than nested screen derivatives of procedural noise. An initial candidate over-filtered textures when the render target flipped the projection's vertical sign; the corrected calculation uses its absolute magnitude. The over-filtered candidate is failure evidence, not a finished grassland result. Material/normal/geometry transitions and high-relief appearance require further native inspection.

**Identities:** application remains 0.0.8; generator 7, geology 3, hydrology 5, climate 3, archive 5. Ecology/forecast/living/seasonal/atmospheric identities retain their earlier values. Experimental flight is an optional traveler field; graphics remain application settings. Earlier worlds require their matching preserved build. No archive is silently rebuilt against changed geography.

## Streaming, visibility and disposable caches

Local tiles retain a bounded radius, full-frustum candidate discovery, LOD hysteresis, lookahead and one local native upload per frame. Background budget selects one to four workers. Regional footprint planning includes the pitched viewport's ground intersections and preserves the current layer during preparation/publication. Geometry levels remain bounded at 256/512/1024 regional cells and a 768-cell overview. No preset holds a 2,048 km world at metre resolution in GPU memory.

Balanced, High Quality and Ultra change actual geometry, texture filtering, local radius, vegetation distance/density, transition time, lookahead, worker count, cached-data memory and shadows. The optional FPS/frame counter and fog controls remain. Cached-data RAM excludes active mesh buffers, native copies, world models and GPU allocations; final resource measurements must report those separately.

Preload Visible Area snapshots current local targets and requests a tagged regional product. Completion counts local meshes only after installation/fade and vegetation placement, and the tagged regional product only after its five-stage native publication. It does not count queued generation as complete. Cancellation resumes normal scheduling and retains installed products. Moving the view cancels the snapshot. Normal movement is always available.

The deterministic presentation cache stores unmodified base terrain payloads, potential vegetation placements and regional height samples. Keys include a rendering ABI, geography fingerprint, generation/environment identities, geometry coordinates and LOD. Files include a format, identity, length and SHA-256. Writes flush a unique temporary file before atomic replacement. Corrupt or incompatible entries reconstruct automatically; cache I/O failure falls back to generation. The in-memory byte LRU is bounded independently from persistent disk storage. Resource depletion, seasonal/ecological state, excavation holes, inventory, objects and archives are not restored from this disposable cache. Sparse runtime changes continue to apply after cached base presentation is installed.

## Atmosphere and navigation

The low cloud sheet is replaced with bounded ray-marched cumulus volumes with rounded lobes and a shared projected terrain-shadow density. Overview weather-system aggregates blend to world-fixed regional formations near the camera, rather than imposing five literal clouds across every physical scale. Density options are Off, Sparse, Normal and Overcast. Placement derives from seed/world coordinates and time; changing settings does not change world state. The maximum is sixteen reusable render bounds during the presentation transition, sharing one cube mesh. This is a volume-rendering approximation, not a new physical meteorology solver. Cloud shapes, scene-depth clipping, transitions, shadows and cost await final player review.

Render Clouds controls geometry and matching shadows. Render Weather Effects independently controls precipitation particles and optional weather-specific fog. Both persist as application settings; climate, precipitation, saved wetness and ecology continue. The existing snow/wetness consequences remain visible environmental state.

Three bounded, angular distant-mountain scenery layers provide a 360-degree ground-view backdrop. They follow camera translation without becoming traversable geography, receive no collision, fade out at overview altitude, and remain behind real nearby surfaces. The final correction gates orbit fading on the active God camera, so a retained overview distance cannot hide Explore scenery. Native ground captures also exposed skybox overwrites of the very distant pass; rendering it after the skybox with depth testing/writing retained removes those hard cutoffs. Their art quality is judged from ground/boundary captures, not inferred from object existence.

Explore mode supports configurable double-Space flight; single Space retains walking jump. Flight uses WASD, held Space/Shift to rise/descend, Ctrl to accelerate, configurable speed and smooth acceleration. Terrain/water clearance and finite world/altitude limits remain enforced even beyond the local collider patch. Landing selects supported safe dry ground; if none exists nearby, flight stays active with an explanation. Flight state participates in traveler capture/clone; no resources are created or consumed. First/third-person behavior uses the existing traveler camera.
