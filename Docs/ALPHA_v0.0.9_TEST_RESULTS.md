## Remaining limitations and incomplete acceptance

- Distant narrow tributary banks and shorelines still reveal coarse sampling. Settled native captures also show angular bank transitions and local visible gaps/mismatches at some river mouths where detailed and regional terrain meet. The Miniature river capture is concrete evidence; this defect is not excused as streaming after the readiness correction. River-mouth continuity/reference-quality acceptance is incomplete. The finer drainage graph can create numerous legitimate small basins; a universal lake-count reduction is not implemented or claimed.
- Terrain, vegetation, Roman character and atmosphere remain stylized procedural art. They do not attain the supplied photographic/game references. Clouds can obscure a benchmark view; raw captures retain that condition.
- Erosion, sediment, climate, snow and cloud behavior use documented physical proxies rather than full fluid/geophysical simulation. Salt-lake classification and spherical-world topology remain future work.
- Cave detail and collision stream around the traveler; the distant view covers the current system. Whole-world underground mesh residency and fully optimized incremental far-cave edit rebuilding are not implemented.
- The Roman rig supports the required eight states; production humanoid assets, an appearance customization UI and detailed facial animation are not implemented in this milestone.
- No independent display-present measurement or universal 60 FPS guarantee is claimed. Frame spikes, generation time and memory must be assessed from the measured tables and raw traces.
- Physical keyboard/display testing, subjective reference-art approval and extended multi-hour soak acceptance remain open. These are carried forward explicitly rather than labeled passed.
- Civilization, settlement, political and social history systems are the subsequent v0.0.10 phase, not v0.0.9 functionality.
